A shareable end-of-drive card, and three bugs its first real drives exposed.

New: when a drive ends you now get a traffic light — green, amber or red — for
each of braking, cornering and acceleration, plus a Share button that hands a
short Wordle-style text card to your share sheet (trip number, score, three
coloured circles; no time or place). Telling those three apart needs to know
which way is forward, which a phone can't know from how it's mounted, so this
build also reads the gyroscope (where the phone has one — still no
permission, still fully offline) and works out the car's own axes from the
drive itself. A phone without a gyroscope falls back to a weaker one-sensor
estimate and shows a grey circle rather than guess when it can't tell.

Fixed, from the first drives recorded with gyroscope data:
- The phone being tilted into its mount at the start could read as a minute
  of phantom acceleration, inflating the acceleration light and hiding
  braking. Gravity is now estimated from the whole drive.
- Picking the phone up mid-drive (for instance when returning to the app) was
  being counted as a burst of harsh braking and cornering. The gyroscope now
  spots the phone being handled and those moments are ignored.
- Stretches where no sensor data arrived were being counted as driving time:
  one 17-minute drive reported 826 seconds of live driving from about 464
  seconds of data. They are no longer scored.

Known issue: Android stops giving sensor data to an app that isn't in front,
so if you switch to a navigation app mid-drive DriveWell goes blind until you
come back. The score covers only the time it could see. Keep DriveWell on
screen for the full picture; a proper fix is being considered.

Still a beta: braking-versus-acceleration relies on the gyroscope and has been
checked against synthetic drives and two real ones, not yet a controlled test.
If a light looks wrong, "Export last drive" is the fastest way to get it looked
at.
