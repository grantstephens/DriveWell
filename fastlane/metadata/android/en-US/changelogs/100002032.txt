Closed a scoring loophole and added a personal-best callout.

- The liveness check that gates points could be fooled by a phone tapped
  every couple of seconds rather than one actually mounted in a running
  vehicle — the tap's strength alone was enough to pass. Points now also
  require the activity to look like continuous engine/road vibration
  rather than sparse, rhythmic taps; a phone left tapped and unattended
  no longer earns points.
- Ending a drive that beats every one of your prior trip scores now shows
  a "new personal best" toast.

Still a beta: scoring constants remain a first calibration pass, not
tuned against real fleets of drives. Feedback on how it feels against
your own driving is very welcome via the issue tracker.
