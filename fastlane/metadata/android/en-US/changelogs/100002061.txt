The real motorway fix, and points are gone.

- Fixed the bug from beta.4/.5's diagnostic data: ordinary motorway
  vibration was scoring careful highway driving at ~70-78% forever, with
  no way to reach a high score even on a genuinely smooth drive. Jerk is
  now scored against an adaptive baseline — "what's been normal for this
  road recently" — instead of a fixed threshold. Real harsh braking or
  cornering still tanks the score exactly as before; sustained ambient
  vibration no longer does.
- Points are gone. The app now shows a live percentage while driving and
  a trip score at the end, plus a lifetime average on Stats — one honest
  number instead of two competing ones.
- A phone left stationary is no longer saved as a fake, unearned 100%
  trip, and no longer inflates your lifetime average or best score.

Heads up: your existing trip history was scored under the old algorithm
(ordinary highway driving typically ~70-78%). New drives will score
noticeably higher under the same conditions — that's the fix working, not
a sudden change in how you drive. Expect an "improvement" trend and maybe
a personal-best toast on your first few drives under this build.

Still a beta: the new baseline's time constant is a first-pass guess, not
tuned against a fleet of real drives. Feedback on how it feels is very
welcome via the issue tracker.
