Initial beta release.

DriveWell scores how smoothly you drive using your phone's accelerometer — no GPS, no
network access, nothing leaves the device. A leaf on the Drive screen greens up as your
driving smooths out, and points accrue once it's fully green. Stats tracks your lifetime
points, best score, total time behind the wheel, and how your last 5 drives compare to
the 5 before them.

This is a beta: the scoring thresholds are a first calibration pass based on
accelerometer physics, not tuned against real fleets of drives yet. Feedback on how the
score feels against your own driving is very welcome via the issue tracker.
