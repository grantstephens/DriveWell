A diagnostic build, not a feature release.

Real motorway driving was pinning the smoothness score around 70% even
with no harsh braking, acceleration, or cornering happening — ambient
road/engine vibration at speed is being misread as rough driving. Rather
than guess at another fix, this build adds a temporary debug capture:
after ending a drive, an "Export debug log" button shares the raw
accelerometer data and the scoring engine's internal state, so the real
fix can be calibrated against an actual drive instead of a synthetic
model.

This debug tooling is temporary and will be removed once the real
scoring fix ships. Not a build to keep long-term.
