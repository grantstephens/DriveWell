Recalibrated scoring, a proper leaf, and a fix for a real gaming loophole.

- Scoring was too sensitive: real-world engine and road vibration was being
  read as harsh driving, dragging the score to 0 even while driving
  carefully. The engine now low-pass filters the signal before judging it,
  which rejects that vibration while still catching genuinely harsh
  braking or cornering.
- The leaf is now built from a proper detailed icon instead of a rough
  hand-drawn shape, with a soft glow that builds as your driving smooths
  out.
- Closed a loophole where leaving the phone completely still (e.g. on a
  table) could rack up points without actually driving anywhere. Points
  now require proof the phone is in an actual moving, running vehicle —
  a stationary phone scores as smooth but earns nothing.

Still a beta: scoring constants are calibrated against synthetic models,
not a fleet of real drives. Feedback on how it feels against your own
driving is very welcome via the issue tracker.
