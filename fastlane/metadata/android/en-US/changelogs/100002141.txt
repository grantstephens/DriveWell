A new icon: a steering wheel whose third spoke grows into a leaf.

The old leaf said "eco" but not "driving". The wheel says driving; the leaf
growing out of it says gently — which is the whole idea behind Featherfoot.
It works as a themed icon too, on phones that tint icons to match the
wallpaper. The leaf inside the app is unchanged: it's the live readout, not
the logo.

That's the only change in this build — the scoring, the traffic lights, the
pause-when-off-screen behaviour and the leaf effects are exactly as in
beta.13. The project's GitHub address has also moved to
github.com/grantstephens/featherfoot (the old address redirects, so existing
links and Obtainium entries keep working), and the share card now points
there.

If you're coming from the old DriveWell app: Featherfoot is a separate
install with its own, empty history, so you can uninstall DriveWell once
you're happy.

Still a beta: if something looks or feels wrong, "Export last drive" in
Settings is the fastest way to get it looked at.
