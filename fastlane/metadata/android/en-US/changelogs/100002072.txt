Fixed the leaf choking itself off the accelerometer.

A real drive's debug export showed the accelerometer's effective sample rate
collapsing from the requested 50 Hz to a sustained ~5 Hz for long stretches
mid-drive — undersampling genuinely harsh braking and acceleration into
invisibility, so a real hard stop could pass with barely a dent in the
score. The cause: every single accelerometer sample was triggering a full
screen re-render, including the leaf's native SVG glow redraw, which was
apparently enough work at 50 Hz to bottleneck the JS thread and starve the
sensor listener itself.

The scoring engine now sees every raw sample same as before; only the
leaf's repaint is capped, to 10 times a second — plenty for something you
glance at while driving.

If you've felt the score stay suspiciously smooth through a drive that
didn't feel smooth at all, this should fix it. A fresh "Export last drive"
from a real drive, compared against one from before this build, is the
best way to confirm — very welcome via the issue tracker either way.

Still a beta: scoring constants remain a first calibration pass, not tuned
against a fleet of real drives.
