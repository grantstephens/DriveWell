Fixes the missing export option from beta.4, and makes it a real feature.

- beta.4's "Export debug log" button never actually appeared on a real
  device — it was on a non-scrolling screen built for three elements, and
  a fourth just got squeezed off-screen. It's been moved to a new "Export
  last drive" card on the Settings screen instead, which fixes that and
  gives it a permanent, discoverable home.
- The export still shares your most recent drive's raw accelerometer data
  and the scoring engine's internal signals — useful for reporting a
  scoring issue or helping tune the algorithm. Nothing is sent
  automatically; you choose where it goes via the share sheet.

If you're on beta.4 specifically to help diagnose the motorway-vibration
scoring issue: update to this build first, then drive your route and use
the new Settings export.
