DriveWell scores how smoothly you drive using nothing but your phone's accelerometer. Harsh acceleration, braking, and cornering drag the score down, and a leaf on the home screen greens up as your driving gets smoother.

It's a game against yourself: once the leaf is green, points rack up with every smooth trip, and your stats track how your driving improves over time.

Everything stays on your phone. No network access, no GPS, no accounts — the sensor data never leaves the device.
