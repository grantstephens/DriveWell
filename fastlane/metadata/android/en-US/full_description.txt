Featherfoot scores how smoothly you drive using nothing but your phone's motion sensors (accelerometer, plus the gyroscope if it has one). Harsh acceleration, braking, and cornering drag the score down, and a leaf on the home screen greens up as your driving gets smoother.

It's a game against yourself: every drive gets a percentage score and a traffic light each for braking, cornering and acceleration — share it like a Wordle result — and your stats track how your driving improves over time.

Everything stays on your phone. No network access, no GPS, no accounts — the sensor data never leaves the device.
